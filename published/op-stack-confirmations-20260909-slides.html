Stage 2 / NotificationsOP Stack · Engineering proposal

Apps still assemble their own confirmation flow.

RPC acknowledgments, sub-block streams and execution results expose different pieces. Each application has to turn them into usable progress.

Let applications subscribe to either confirmation, or both.

Ordering confirmation

Is my transaction in the sequencer’s order?

Notify the application of its provisional position.

Execution confirmation

Did it succeed, and what changed?

Provide receipt status and logs, with the resulting state available through RPC.

Cover reverted transactions, transactions without logs, revocations and recovery after reconnects. These signals may arrive together; neither implies finality.

Stage 2 outcome

An application can follow one transaction without polling.

Stage 3 / PerformanceOP Stack · Engineering proposal

Fast sub-blocks still leave transactions waiting.

Transport, buffering and scheduling can delay a transaction before it reaches the next sub-block. The baseline should tell us which waits are worth removing.

Start with avoidable waits on the route to the builder.

Candidate: eliminate mempool P2P from the critical path.
